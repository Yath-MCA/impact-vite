About IMPACT

Worried about the review and approval process?

Say good-bye to the hectic process. Yes, it’s time to drink our own champagne. Newgen presents Online proofing tool to take up your burden and make you stress-free.

What is IMPACT?

The goal of our platform is to simplify and expedite collaborations. With a few steps, IMPACT accurately flatten the complex review and approval process and effectuate the PDF with just one click.

Why IMPACT is unique?

Our IMPACT has changed the game of traditional review process by offering fast, transparent, accurate and productive method. With one tool, complete the entire process with ease and with one click, get your reviewed PDF ready.

What do we do for you?

We offer sophisticated endorsement editing and workflow functionalities to save time and improve real-time collaboration between Authors and Editors, thus results in quickened and collaborative progress even for complex files. Our user-friendly editing features allow you to review and comment in a more easier way than any other feature can do.
Our intuitive tool helps you to quickly acquire accurate feedback without frustrating internal and external stakeholders. Work with our IMPACT to swiftly eliminate extra steps.

Want to make workflow easier?

Our online proofing tool provides excellent and various workflow functionalities to make more complex and entangled processes easy for everyone. Highly efficient workflows speed up the approval process and boost the quality of your work in a timely manner.

What you can do with IMPACT?

Add comment, raise the query, request changes, collaborate in real-time and move to the next cycle automatically without any delay. No waiting! Get your reviewed PDF of books/journals without further ado.
We value your every second, so we created IMPACT as fast as possible. We understand your comfort, so we shaped IMPACT in ease to use way. We comprehend your interest, so we designed in a more elegant way.
